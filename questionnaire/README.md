# Questionnaire

## Disclaimer

This questionnaire is anonymous and aims to evaluate a VSCode plugin for pull request management.
This survey is conducted by students and researchers at the University of Namur, under the supervision of Professors Benoît Vanderose and Xavier Devroey from the SNAIL team (contact: snail.info@unamur.be).
